Reset the history of {{ location.name }}?

This cannot be undone. Resetting the history:

  • deletes all recorded power history of this location: the chart and the daily totals show no data for the time before the reset, and Recent outages is empty;
  • unpins its weekly chart in the channel if the bot can still pin there; otherwise unpin it by hand in Telegram (the posted messages stay in the channel);
  • sets it to Waiting for first heartbeat: nothing is detected until the next heartbeat, which restarts monitoring as On without an alert and posts and pins a new chart;
  • still sends the alerts already queued, because they report real events;
  • keeps the settings, the device key, the switches and the Delivery status.

To remove a single false outage instead, use Recent outages on the location page.
