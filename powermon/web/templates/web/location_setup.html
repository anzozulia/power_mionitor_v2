    1. 1

      Before you start

      Use a device that loses power in a blackout: plugged into mains, with no UPS, power bank or battery. A device on backup power keeps reporting and hides the outage.

      A heartbeat needs both power and internet at the device. If the router or the provider's network is down, this location is reported OFF too.

      The device must send a heartbeat at least every {{ period_s }} s. Power is reported OFF after {{ off_after_s }} s without one.
