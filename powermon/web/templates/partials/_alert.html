Parameters: tone (success, info, warning, error, muted), title (optional, in the tone's text colour), body (optional), testid (optional data-testid), role (only when passed: "alert" for inline form errors and throttle messages; alerts that are not live messages have no role), action_template (optional: the name of a template included in the action slot, e.g. a fix button; it sees the including context) and action_testid (optional: the action's testid).
